A note, becoming knowledge

Why Skriuw

Writing should not wait on the machinery.

Immediate

Thought in. Words on screen.

Editing and navigation happen locally, so opening a note never waits on a server, disk round-trip, or network request.

Private

Local is the default.

Your workspace stays on your device. Sync and remote AI remain off until you explicitly choose to use them.

Recoverable

Your work has a way home.

Verified backups, portable exports, trash recovery, and background history keep mistakes from becoming endings.

A serious notebook

Rich enough for the work.
Quiet enough for the words.

Skriuw combines the freedom of Markdown with the structure of a knowledge base, without turning writing into database maintenance.

Write your way

Rich text and raw Markdown, tables, code, checklists, slash commands, and templates.

Connect the useful parts

Wikilinks, backlinks, tags, people, typed properties, and a relationship explorer.

Keep momentum

Keyboard-first navigation, command palette, tabs, split view, and instant search.

Own the archive

Local SQLite storage, Markdown import and export, Git-backed history, and verified backups.

Private by default

Nothing syncs unless you say so.

Start locally with no account and no telemetry. If you later want the same workspace on another device, sign in and enable sync on your terms.
