# Tasks

These example tasks are linked to this note. Open **Tasks** in the sidebar to see them grouped by due date. Clicking a task's source takes you back here.

## Try the checklist

- [x] Open your workspace
- [ ] Write a note about something on your mind 📅 {{today}}
- [ ] Review that note tomorrow 📅 {{tomorrow}}
- [ ] Try adding a task of your own

Tick a checkbox here and its task completes in Tasks. Edit its text and the task title follows. You can also change the date from Tasks.

## Add your own

Choose `/task` on an empty line, or type `- ` followed by `[ ] ` and then a title. Press Enter after a task to continue with another linked task.

On a checklist line, type `due:tomorrow` followed by a space to add a date. `due:today`, weekdays such as `due:friday`, and dates such as `due:2027-01-15` work too. Click a date chip to edit it, or delete the chip to clear it.

## Checklists and tasks

Ordinary checklists imported from Markdown stay inside their note. They appear in Tasks when you explicitly turn them into tasks. [[Launch checklist]] is an ordinary checklist you can use to explore that distinction.

Due dates export as a calendar sign followed by a date, such as `📅 2027-01-15`. Linked tasks also carry a private Markdown marker so their source survives export.

These are disposable examples. You can delete them from Tasks and write your own.
