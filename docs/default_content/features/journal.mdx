# Journal

Skiruw's Journal is a dedicated space for daily writing — one entry per day, separate from your regular notes, with its own calendar, moods, and tags.

## How it works

Pick a day from the calendar and start typing — your words save automatically as you go. Each day gets exactly one entry, so returning to a date always brings you back to what you wrote there. Today's entry opens with a friendly heading ("Today", "Yesterday", "Tomorrow", or the weekday name) instead of a plain date.

## Getting around

- **Calendar view** — browse by month, jump forward or back, and see a dot on any day that already has an entry
- **Jump to date** — pick any date directly from a date picker
- **Today button** — always one click back to today's entry
- **Search** — find entries by their text or tags
- **All entries** — a chronological list of everything you've written
- **Tags view** — browse entries grouped by tag

## Moods and tags

- Mark how you're feeling with a quick mood picker (Great, Good, Neutral, Low, Rough) — shown right on the entry and in the calendar
- Add `@tags` to categorize entries, then browse or filter by them later
- Give an entry a custom title, or leave it blank and Skiruw uses the start of your text

## Tips

- Switch between rich text and plain text writing modes
- See your current and longest writing streaks, mood breakdowns, and a 30-day activity heatmap in the Stats view
- Export your whole journal as Markdown, JSON, or a printable PDF for backup or sharing
- Word count and an "auto-saved" indicator sit quietly at the bottom of each entry

## Why use the Journal?

Regular notes are for ideas and reference; the Journal is for the daily record — what happened, how you felt, what you're tracking over time. Because it's separate from your notes but built on the same editor, you get a clean, calendar-driven writing habit without cluttering your note tree.
